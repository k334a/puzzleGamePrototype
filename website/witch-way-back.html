The Story

Studying some higher-level spells on your own, you accidentally turned yourself into a cat! Whoops. None of your classes prepared you for this unfortunate circumstance.

Luckily, as a cat, you can still perform spells from your spell book if you find them again. Maybe there are some things you'll do easier as a smaller, cuter creature.

Spells

❄️
Freeze Spell
Keeps your paws dry and grants access to fast-flowing waterfalls. Levels requiring this spell often hide a secret ingredient — combine it with other magical items to craft the Humanizer Potion!
✦ BASIC
💨
Wind Spell
Lets a cat soar through the air with grace — or so they say. This is a rare spell, and for good reason. It is difficult to master, but those who do are greatly rewarded.
◆ RARE
✨
Light Spell
Light up your surroundings to find your way around dark spaces. You may be a cat now but you aren't so good at seeing in the dark like one.
◆ BASIC
🌿
Plant Spell
Grow plants quickly with the power of magic. Who knows when a spell like this may come in handy.
◆ RARE

How To Play

1
Explore each level
Navigate as a cat through magical environments. Use WSAD to move, I for inventory. Spells fall into slots Z, X, C, and V
2
Collect spells
Walk into scrolls to collect them. Each spell unlocks a new ability, and it may take a little time to learn their most effective use-case.
3
Collect ingredients
Hidden items are scattered throughout the world. These magical items could be easily missed, but it's imperative you get all 4 - you miss your human form.
4
Brew the Humanizer Potion
Combine your gathered ingredients and find your way back to your true form. You've got this, little witch!

The Goal

Humanizer Potion
A legendary concoction that reverses even the most catastrophic of self-inflicted transfigurations. You'll need to collect rare magical items scattered across each level and combine them just right.
